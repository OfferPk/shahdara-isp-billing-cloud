Sign in to your account

Customers sign in with their PPPoE username and a separately provisioned portal password. Shahdara staff sign in with their assigned username and password. Customer portal passwords are read-only here; self-service sign-up is disabled.

Username and password sign-in

Internal staging accounts use a separately assigned portal password; their PPPoE password is not changed by portal sign-in.

Private customer and billing records are protected by Supabase Auth and database row-level security. Customer portal access is issued by an authorized Shahdara administrator.
